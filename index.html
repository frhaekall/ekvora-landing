Frequently Asked Questions.

Find answers to common questions about using EKVORA.

How do I download a video using EKVORA?
Copy the video link from Instagram, TikTok, or Facebook. Paste it into the EKVORA input field, click Process Video, then preview and download the available video.
Which platforms does EKVORA support?
EKVORA currently supports publicly accessible video links from Instagram, TikTok, and Facebook.
Do I need to create an account?
No. You can use the downloader directly without creating an account.
Why can't my video be processed?
Make sure the URL is correct and the video is publicly accessible. Some links may fail because of platform restrictions or temporary service issues.
Why does my download link expire?
The generated download link may only be valid for a limited time. If it expires, paste the original social media link into EKVORA and process it again.
Can I download private videos?
EKVORA is intended for publicly accessible videos. It does not guarantee access to private or restricted content.
